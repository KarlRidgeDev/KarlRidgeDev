## About my training

I am enrolled on Think Employment's **Level 3 Skills Bootcamp in Cyber Security**, a 12–16 week online programme aimed at developing practical skills for entry-level cyber security work. My own career goal is to progress towards penetration testing. The programme promotes hands-on use of tools and portfolio projects; this page documents **my own learning and practical exercises**, rather than reproducing course materials.

## Topics I have studied

- **Cybersecurity fundamentals:** CIA triad, authentication and authorisation, least privilege, vulnerabilities and common threat types.
- **Networks and defensive controls:** Ports and protocols, firewalls, malware, phishing, denial-of-service attacks and protective measures.
- **Incident response:** SANS incident response phases, planning for incidents, and reviewing a phishing scenario through a post-mortem exercise.
- **OSINT and investigations:** Public-information sources, interpreting evidence, false positives and negatives, and ethical limitations.
- **Security frameworks and management:** NIST, NCSC guidance, CIS Controls, ISO 27001, risk assessment and security by design.
- **Legislation and professional practice:** UK GDPR, the Computer Misuse Act, cyber law, ethics, screening and continuous professional development.

These topics reflect coursework and learning activities I have worked through. They are not a claim that every module, assessment or qualification has been completed.
